    Your first winter

      If you don’t hear back

      • Response times vary. Follow up, and try email, phone and an in-person visit together if it’s urgent.
      • Not sure who to ask? Call or text 211, or visit RODS (2314 11th Avenue) if you’re eligible.

      About, privacy & terms

      About this site

      Regina Student Life is a free, non-commercial directory made by an individual student in Regina to help new students find support. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with any school, the City of Regina, RODS or any listed organization, and it sells nothing. Contact: reginastudentlife.help@gmail.com.

      Privacy

      This site has no accounts, forms, ads or analytics, and it does not collect or store your personal information on a server. What you type in the search box stays in your browser. Your tick-box progress on the checklists is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and can be cleared in your browser settings. If you email us, we use your message only to reply or fix the site. The site’s host (Netlify) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses, as most websites do. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

      Cookies & third parties

      We set no cookies of our own. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page opens. The translation feature is optional: only when you pick a language does the page load Google Translate, which sets a cookie (googtrans) to remember your choice and sends the page text to Google to translate it. Choosing English clears that cookie. Links to other websites (schools, government, Reddit and service sites) are covered by their own privacy policies.

      Terms of use & disclaimers

      This site is for general information only. It is not legal, immigration, medical, financial or mental health advice and does not replace professional help. Information may be out of date or wrong, so confirm hours, prices, eligibility and contact details with each service before relying on it. We cannot guarantee that any service will be available or right for you, and we don’t control other websites or services. The site is free, with no payments, so no refunds apply. Corrections are welcome by email. If you are in danger or crisis, call 911 or 9-8-8.

      Accessibility

      We aim to make this site usable with a keyboard, screen readers and phones, with readable colour contrast and light and dark modes. If something doesn’t work for you, email us and we’ll fix it.

      Sources

      Content comes from a Regina new-student guide, school and government pages, 211 Saskatchewan, local news and public student discussion. Figures such as rents and vacancy rates are approximate and change.
